## Legacy QtPi Apps

These apps are kept for historical reference.

They are not the source of truth for the current ESP32 QtPi stack.

### Subfolders

- [veda-atmega](/Users/nemmart/antony/qtpi/repos/code2play/qtpi-shared/apks/legacy/veda-atmega)
  older shared QtPi apps currently classified as Veda/ATmega-era
- [rio-atmega](/Users/nemmart/antony/qtpi/repos/code2play/qtpi-shared/apks/legacy/rio-atmega)
  placeholder for Rio/ATmega-era apps when identified with confidence

### Why They Are Legacy

The moved apps were inspected and all share an older App Inventor format profile:
- `YaVersion=208`
- `Form $Version=27`

That is materially older than the current QtPi/App Inventor stack used for ESP32 validation.
